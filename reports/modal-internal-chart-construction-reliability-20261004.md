## Bounded cold internal policy

`search_canonical_direction_inward` is a separate test-only entry. It validates
the current request's normalized direction, moves the uniquely dominant anchor
one f64 step toward zero before fitting, and freezes that new anchor. A tie or
near-tie that loses unique dominance fails before fitting. It uses unit weights
because these coordinates are already mass-normalized, not physical mass
reweighting or forced unit-shape normalization.

The internal order set remains grid-norm then reverse, with at most two fits
and thirteen operator certificates. Faults, malformed certificates, cancellation
or a lost final certificate stop immediately; they cannot activate another fit.
Only typed intermediate numerical rejection permits the second order. It never
follows the original-chart policy as an extra retry, borrows an old direction or
uses recipe identity to choose a policy.

The existing physical one-fit inward chart shares the chart preparation helper
but retains its original unit-shape contract and single checked-order plan.
The old physical and holdout regressions remain separately asserted. No code
leaves Solver's test-only ancestry; Engine, Agent and public Solver admission
