## Frozen chart diagnosis

The original physical grid fixes its largest mass-weighted amplitude coordinate
and optimizes only the remaining coordinates. The layered 128-member chain at
coordinate scale 1e-10 rejects uniform `(Young, density)` multipliers `(1, 4)`
and `(16, 4)`. Original-chart handoff, ranked and full cold routes all retain
a best physical residual of 1.0241712231970532e-8, above the unchanged 1e-8 gate.

For each counterexample, nine separate physical chart fits use fixed anchor
offsets `0, -1, +1, -2, +2, -4, +4, -8, +8` in adjacent f64 steps. Both have
five accepted charts, at `-1, +1, -2, +4, -8`; the other four remain rejected.
Every fit starts from the same current-request physical seed and constructs its
own factor and certificate. They are isolated diagnostic alternatives, not an
eighteen-fit runtime search or eighteen freshly rebuilt requests.

The positive anchor is coordinate 252. The new seed and the old-material
reference-only witness differ there by 175 representable steps. The witness is
printed only for diagnosis and never supplied to a search. Moving one step,
not copying the witness coordinate, already permits accepted physical directions.
This identifies a restrictive search chart on these inputs; it is not proof
that every failure is caused by the anchor or that one-step movement always works.

## Single fit inward chart

`search_canonical_unit_shape_inward` is a separate cold candidate entry under
the existing test-only grid ancestor. Before any factorization it moves the
largest mass-weighted anchor exactly one representable step toward zero.
That new coordinate is then frozen throughout the sole physical fit.

The policy depends on current numeric input, not profile identity, coordinate
scale, changed-material labels, old directions or successful reference output.
The checked internal order tag still selects the physical fitting order, without
transferring its permutation, matrix, factor or residual certificate. Both
positive and negative directions use the same sign-symmetric inward rule.

Preflight rejects tied or near-tied anchors if moving inward loses unique
dominance. It also retains range, dimensional, mass and strict unit-norm checks.
It does not renormalize accepted output. Original seeds and frozen eigenvalues
are immutable. An operator fault is propagated, never parsed as a retryable
numerical rejection. There is no original-chart attempt followed by inward retry.

## Bounds and failure controls

The physical chart reserves one fit and at most seven certificate callbacks.
At 256 active coordinates its conservative candidate reservation is
6,946,816 payload bytes and 338,198,528 component visits. This includes extra
chart validation/copy work, 196,608 more reserved visits than the old one-fit
physical chart. All 52 successful cases observe four physical certificates.
An observer independently counts one physical QR factor per successful request.

The internal ranked stage still reserves two fits/thirteen certificates.
Summed grid-only caps are three fits/twenty certificates and 1,011,744,768 visits.
Those are conservative reservations, not measured operations, elapsed timings
or complete heap/whole-pipeline budgets. The sum still exceeds the ordinary
350-million proposal boundary. No ignored benchmark campaign is run or newly
qualified by this follow-up, and previous timing regressions remain relevant.

Eighteen synthetic sign/permutation controls check chart mechanics for all three
orders, including the exact seven-certificate worst-case counter. They do not
constitute physical accuracy evidence. Malformed certificates, invalid masses,
range/norm errors, anchor ties, final certificate deterioration and faults fail
closed. Four synthetic cancellation points cover chart preparation, QR setup,
first search certificate and last chart validation, with exact fresh replays.

Both real counterexamples additionally cover four signed physical JSON
readbacks, two injected physical rejections, two final operator faults and two
last-original-node restoration cancellations. No partial result is returned;
two complete fresh request replays preserve exact mapped output and seeds.
