What daji 3.x is optimizing for

Early Daji is hardening an agent-driven industrial research system, not maximizing feature count. At the current 3.4.5 checkpoint, progress means complete research journeys: bounded objectives, real execution, validated results, safe recovery, and reproducible evidence. Industrial reliability is the goal; each claim still requires its own proof.

Daji 3.4.5 checkpoint

  • Physical tridiagonal preparation validates symmetry and range once; cancellation and preparation errors cannot silently become fallback.
  • Modal vector arithmetic, refinement and final validation gain bounded cooperative checks. Failed later modes discard private earlier candidates.
  • Nontridiagonal single-mode problems up to 256 active degrees of freedom receive complete-spectrum admission and bounded coordinate correction, retaining the eigenvalue and 1e-8 physical residual gate.
  • Rust Headless and development Agent-stage regressions exercise failure isolation and replay; Solver retains numerical ownership.
  • Multimode, projection, coherent-phase and quantized-QR comparisons remain test-only. Independent physical acceptance does not promote them to public runtime fallbacks.
  • Existing graph/artifact admission, compact modeling, bundle creation, PWDT, and language-pack capabilities remain available with their original qualification limits.

Source and package metadata now target daji 3.4.5. This patch does not rebuild or install binaries; the last local desktop and runtime baseline remains 3.4.0. Scoped numerical regressions do not establish external-solver correlation, general physical qualification, Windows, installed-Agent, GUI, or scale acceptance. The tiny-scale 128-element public solve still fails closed. Recompute affected historical modal results; tensor gaps remain open.

Agent-driven, with human control

Research agents use the Rust, Python, or Elixir Headless SDKs to orchestrate studies. Rust Agents independently execute admitted tasks through their local engines. Models cannot authorize their own plans, bypass resource limits, or turn an unvalidated result into evidence.

The acceptance loop is objective, capability discovery, validated workflow, authorized execution, observation and recovery, numerical review, then evidence export or a bounded next round. Workbench supports modeling, review, and human takeover of the same backend state. Pwdt automates the GUI but is not required for Headless operation.

Line posture

  • Keep major version posture conservative.
  • Prefer verified solver growth over speculative UI inflation.
  • Make release and automation behavior explicit instead of folklore-driven.
  • Carry forward open qualification work without promoting historical evidence to Daji.

What this means in practice

First harden one Installer-managed research journey, then deepen its numerical trust, failure recovery, replay, human review, and measured performance. Security and caller-owned approval apply throughout. Add new capabilities when they remove demonstrated blockers; do not lower tensor targets or platform release gates to claim completion.
