What daji 3.x is optimizing for

Early Daji is hardening an agent-driven industrial research system, not maximizing feature count. At the current 3.4.4 checkpoint, progress means complete research journeys: bounded objectives, real execution, validated results, safe recovery, and reproducible evidence. Industrial reliability is the goal; each claim still requires its own proof.

Daji 3.4.4 checkpoint

  • Modal assembly and shared mass normalization retain representable weak couplings and reject invalid range loss before publication.
  • Bounded inverse refinement, chain recovery and physical shape validation keep the original residual and convergence gates.
  • Exceptional sparse products avoid intermediate underflow/overflow; ordinary products keep their original arithmetic order.
  • Agent TaskIR admits only bar and planar/spatial modal built-ins through Engine. Numeric JSON, authority, cancellation and same-connection replay receive local live TCP regressions.
  • Native development-cache management reclaims allowlisted artifacts without deleting dependencies, source or installed applications.
  • Existing graph/artifact admission, compact modeling, bundle creation, PWDT, and language-pack capabilities remain available with their original qualification limits.

Source and package metadata now target daji 3.4.4. This patch does not rebuild or install binaries; the last local desktop and runtime baseline remains 3.4.0. Scoped numerical regressions do not establish external-solver correlation, general physical qualification, Windows, installed-Agent, GUI, or scale acceptance. The retained 128-element bending case still fails closed. Recompute affected historical modal results; tensor gaps remain open.

Agent-driven, with human control

Research agents use the Rust, Python, or Elixir Headless SDKs to orchestrate studies. Rust Agents independently execute admitted tasks through their local engines. Models cannot authorize their own plans, bypass resource limits, or turn an unvalidated result into evidence.

The acceptance loop is objective, capability discovery, validated workflow, authorized execution, observation and recovery, numerical review, then evidence export or a bounded next round. Workbench supports modeling, review, and human takeover of the same backend state. Pwdt automates the GUI but is not required for Headless operation.

Line posture

  • Keep major version posture conservative.
  • Prefer verified solver growth over speculative UI inflation.
  • Make release and automation behavior explicit instead of folklore-driven.
  • Carry forward open qualification work without promoting historical evidence to Daji.

What this means in practice

First harden one Installer-managed research journey, then deepen its numerical trust, failure recovery, replay, human review, and measured performance. Security and caller-owned approval apply throughout. Add new capabilities when they remove demonstrated blockers; do not lower tensor targets or platform release gates to claim completion.
