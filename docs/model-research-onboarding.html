Required sequence

  1. Ingest the bootstrap documents and hard rules.
  2. Verify service health and protocol compatibility.
  3. Project only policy-allowed tools for the selected provider.
  4. Normalize one dependency frontier into a portable proposal.
  5. Build the Headless plan and obtain exact caller approval.
  6. Dispatch through the selected SDK bridge and retain its execution receipt.
  7. Verify the receipt and advance the bound cross-turn research frontier.
  8. Observe, fetch, validate, and retain research evidence.

Dependency rule

Never invent future job ids. Submit first, consume the real receipt, then plan wait and result-fetch calls in later model turns.

Native execution bridge

Rust, Python, and Elixir reject missing or mismatched caller approvals before network access. A caller-owned verifier authenticates approval independently of model output, and the receipt distinguishes completed execution from partial failure. A separate caller-owned receipt verifier gates frontier advancement and preserves the real job binding across later turns. A frontier verifier separately authenticates persisted state before it can generate another call.

What counts as research

A retained result must expose execution authority, provenance, validation evidence, quality gates, limitations, reproducibility, and next validation actions. A plan or real solve alone is not qualification.
