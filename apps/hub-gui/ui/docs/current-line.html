daji 3.3.0

Early Daji prioritizes hardening an agent-driven industrial research system: bounded studies, real execution, numerical trust, safe recovery, and reproducible evidence rather than a larger feature catalog.

The current development point is daji 3.3.0. Local desktop and runtime packaging also targets daji 3.3.0; public distribution remains a separate, evidence-gated action. Daji succeeds the moxi 2.x development line; historical qualification keeps its original version and scope.

This is the short Hub mirror of the central chapter docs/book-ch02-version-line.html.

What this line means

  • Keep the inherited baseline contract-driven instead of returning to prototype sprawl.
  • Prefer confidence and coherence over raw feature-count inflation.
  • Research agents use Headless SDKs; Rust Agents execute admitted tasks. Neither bypasses caller-owned approval or quality gates.
  • Keep Workbench first-class for modeling, review, and human takeover; do not require GUI setup for Headless research.
  • Expand capabilities when they remove a demonstrated research-loop blocker.

Current 3.3.0 checkpoint

  • The modeling viewport brings setup, modeling, inline save, execution, and results together; fullscreen retains its own compact tools.
  • Hub bundle creation has a focused flow and native directory selection.
  • PWDT has a dedicated workspace, embedded editor, and grouped execution and inspection panels.
  • Material-library, runtime, and security controls use compact panel grouping.
  • Local language-pack repairs remove mixed-language copy; Arabic, Persian, and Spanish PWDT each cover 175 panel, DSL, and catalog entries.
  • Translation gaps remain in SDK-bridge copy, generated summaries, diagnostics, and other locale surfaces. Thirty available packs do not mean complete translation.
  • The coverage tensor keeps its dated calibration and open scenarios; this documentation update adds no new numerical or installed-release qualification.
  • GUI and backend actions must still close complete, recoverable research journeys. New packages need their own platform checks.
