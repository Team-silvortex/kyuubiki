daji 3.4.7

Early Daji prioritizes hardening an agent-driven industrial research system: bounded studies, real execution, numerical trust, safe recovery, and reproducible evidence rather than a larger feature catalog.

The current development point is daji 3.4.7. Source package metadata targets 3.4.5; the last locally built and installed desktop/runtime baseline remains daji 3.4.0. This patch does not rebuild or install binaries; public distribution remains a separate, evidence-gated action. Daji succeeds the moxi 2.x development line; historical qualification keeps its original version and scope.

What this line means

  • Keep the inherited baseline contract-driven instead of returning to prototype sprawl.
  • Prefer confidence and coherence over raw feature-count inflation.
  • Research agents use Headless SDKs; Rust Agents execute admitted tasks. Neither bypasses caller-owned approval or quality gates.
  • Keep Workbench first-class for modeling, review, and human takeover; do not require GUI setup for Headless research.
  • Expand capabilities when they remove a demonstrated research-loop blocker.

Current 3.4.7 checkpoint

  • Tridiagonal preparation validates physical symmetry and range once; errors and cancellation cannot silently become fallback.
  • Vector arithmetic, refinement and final physical validation use bounded cancellation checks. Earlier private modes are discarded if a later mode fails.
  • Nontridiagonal single-mode problems up to 256 active degrees of freedom use complete-spectrum admission and bounded coordinate correction, retaining the eigenvalue and 1e-8 physical residual gate.
  • Rust Headless and development Agent-stage tests cover failure isolation and replay. Numerical algorithms remain in Solver.
  • Multimode, projection, phase and quantized-QR proposals remain test-only. The tiny-scale 128-element public solve still fails closed; test-only success is not public or installed qualification.
  • Recompute affected modal results. The following capabilities retain their earlier evidence scope; this patch adds no Linux, installed-platform or scale qualification.
  • Truss, thermal-truss, beam, and frame operators check assembly and recovered fields, including fixed systems and arithmetic extremes.
  • Planar bending-field energy and spatial thermal-energy cancellation are corrected; support bases and recovered reactions are stabilized.
  • Spatial section hints preserve an orthonormal, right-handed basis and equivalent fields without changing the default reference-axis rule.
  • Public Solver and Rust Headless plan/bridge/Engine regressions cover analytical fields, orientation, refinement, cancellation, and fresh replay on macOS and Linux.
  • Recompute affected historical results. These tests are not new external-solver, general physics, Windows, installed-Agent, GUI, or scale qualification.
  • Workflow admission/deletion commit job and result together under Orchestra ownership. Failed writes preserve the previous pair.
  • Result administration rejects private recovery injection, retains workflow identity, and does not recreate missing receipts.
  • CST orientation, Q4/tetrahedral geometry, and thermal-plane output receive kernel and headless regressions. Recompute historical clockwise/mixed CST results before research reuse.
  • Paired Linux kernel benchmarks are scoped measurements, not whole-solve performance claims.
  • Rust and Elixir share condition and named-input routing contracts. Full-snapshot job updates reject stale progress.
  • Solver results and completion publish atomically; workflow runtime, recovery envelopes, and job transitions also commit together.
  • Memory jobs/results share one integrity-checked generation. Scoped storage outages preserve watchdog/coordinator availability and expose degraded health with unknown counts.
  • Workflow graphs pass bounded topology and JSON validation before execution. Operator results pass publication checks before downstream consumption.
  • Explicit branch skipping preserves failure receipts; deterministic diagnostics and shared Rust/Elixir fixtures strengthen replay consistency.
  • CI repairs cover split metric aliases, real quality-score output, terminal job receipts, and a bounded pre-page browser startup retry.
  • The modeling viewport brings setup, modeling, inline save, execution, and results together; fullscreen retains its own compact tools.
  • Hub bundle creation has a focused flow and native directory selection.
  • PWDT has a dedicated workspace, embedded editor, and grouped execution and inspection panels.
  • Material-library, runtime, and security controls use compact panel grouping.
  • Local language-pack repairs remove mixed-language copy; Arabic, Persian, and Spanish PWDT each cover 175 panel, DSL, and catalog entries.
  • Translation gaps remain in SDK-bridge copy, generated summaries, diagnostics, and other locale surfaces. Thirty available packs do not mean complete translation.
  • The coverage tensor retains open scenarios; scoped solver and storage regressions do not establish all-physics, large-scale, or installed-release qualification.
  • GUI and backend actions must still close complete, recoverable research journeys. New packages need their own platform checks.
